Each buddy uses AI to ideate their subsystem position before Zoom. Version B: Live Zoom-updated version. Design decisions are updated during the Zoom discussion through challenge, negotiation, and constraint response. Version C: Final AI-vetted gap check. After discussion, AI is used as a reviewer to identify missing assumptions, risks, tests, or unclear decisions. Marks are not awarded for blindly following AI. Marks are awarded for critical evaluation and human engineering judgement ; meaning, I want to see you guys discuss during zoom. A change without justification is weak evidence. •  Every buddy must contribute at least one challenge, defence, trade-off, or correction. •  Any use of AI during the Zoom session must be screen-shared and visible to all participants at the point it is being discussed. •  If AI-generated content is introduced during the live discussion, the team must explain the prompt used, the output received, and why the output is being considered. •  AI suggestions must be challenged, validated, refined, or rejected through human engineering discussion; “AI said so” is not sufficient justification. Acceptable evidence examples:  GitHub commit link, short test log, video timestamp, oscilloscope or serial monitor screenshot, pin test result, task timing measurement, AI prompt and critique, meeting decision note, or photo of hardware wiring. AI-use declaration:  Do not ask AI to complete this template on behalf of the team. Use AI as a reviewer, challenger, or brainstorming partner. The team confirms that all final entries were reviewed, corrected, and accepted by the team, and that every member can explain the decisions related to their subsystem.
